# DHS Africa

DHS Africa (DH Solutions Africa) builds the digital infrastructure for hospitals and health systems in Sub-Saharan Africa, with engineering in Munich and its own teams in Kenya, Uganda, Nigeria and Rwanda.

**Meridian by DHS Africa** is our hospital management system for hospitals, clinic groups and pharmacies: clinical work, billing, insurance claims and Ministry of Health reporting on one patient record. Certified by Kenya's Digital Health Agency and connected to Uganda's national DHIS2.

**DHS Clinical AI** is the first clinical AI system for Sub-Saharan Africa, built with universities and hosted inside the country.
